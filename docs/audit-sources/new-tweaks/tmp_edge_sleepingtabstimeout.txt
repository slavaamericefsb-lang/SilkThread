SleepingTabsTimeout Feedback Summarize this article for me In this article Set the background tab inactivity timeout for sleeping tabs Supported versions Windows: ≥ 88 macOS: ≥ 88 Android: Not supported iOS: Not supported Description This policy setting lets you configure the timeout, in seconds, after which inactive background tabs are automatically put to sleep if sleeping tabs is enabled. By default, this timeout is 900 seconds (15 minutes). When energy saver is active, Microsoft Edge uses the shorter of this timeout and the energy saver timeout. The energy saver timeout is 5 minutes in maximum savings mode. In other active modes, it's 30 minutes, or 15 minutes on devices with less than 6 GB of memory. Tabs are only put to sleep automatically when the policy SleepingTabsEnabled is enabled or isn't configured, and the user has enabled the sleeping tabs setting. If you don't configure this policy, users can choose the timeout value.
